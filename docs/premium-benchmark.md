## What a $3,000 buyer would evaluate

The target buyer is an internal tools or SaaS team building agent-assisted operations. A credible pilot measures time from installing the kit to implementing one authenticated workflow, with approval, evidence, reconnection, and failure handling. At a hypothetical blended rate of $150 per hour, a $3,000 purchase would need to save 20 hours to break even on engineering time alone. Measure that with a buyer pilot before using the price as a market claim.

Before selling at that tier, close the gaps buyers actually need in the pilot. Candidates include a paired Figma library, a framework adapter with a connected reference backend, documented accessibility and browser coverage, and a clear license, support policy, and update cadence. The current deliverable gives the buyer a working operations reference and a code integration surface to evaluate.
